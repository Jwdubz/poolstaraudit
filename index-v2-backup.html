What I found

01

The homepage is slow in the load test.

In two runs of a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 5.6 and 6.2 seconds and its largest part, a background image section, at 12.5 and 13.1 seconds. The browser was also tied up for about 150 milliseconds, and the page is about 1.6 MB. The first two errors the browser logged were a chat-widget script from cdn.apigateway.co that was blocked by cross-origin rules.

02

Your address and hours differ between the page and its markup.

Your pages show a PO Box in Henderson (PO Box 530505, Henderson, NV 89053) and no hours. Your business markup says 8325 Bomberos Ct, Las Vegas, NV 89113, open Monday to Friday, 6 am to 6 pm.

03

Six of the nine pages I saved have no description, and some titles and headings are off.

The About, Posts, Superior Standards, Our Reviews, FAQs and Save 50% pages have no page description. The About page is titled “About Poolstar™ 2”. The Contact page's main heading is “About Poolstar™”. The Save 50% page has no main heading, and its “limited time offer” has no end date. Your pool service guide is titled “(2025)”.

04

The price match is stated two ways.

Your Superior Standards page, Posts page and pool service guide say you will price match for the first month. Your Las Vegas Pool Service FAQs page, which isn't in your menu, says “price matching for up to three months”.

05

The Our Reviews page has no reviews on it.

It has 103 words and logo links. It says “Google+”, which Google closed in 2019. The Google link is a search address with Pakistan language and country settings (hl=en-PK, gl=pk), and one Yelp link still uses http.

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your homepage 90. It found white text on an orange button that measured 2.9 for contrast, 7 links with no readable name and 1 heading out of order. Your call links are written “tel:(702) 557-6625”, with brackets and a space. I didn't test them.

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the contact form works, the reviews on Google and Yelp, and speed on pages other than the homepage.
